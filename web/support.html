Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the recycling identification?

Our AI recognition system is continuously improving and currently has an accuracy rate of approximately 85-90% for common household items. However, accuracy may vary depending on lighting conditions, item positioning, and whether the item is in our database. Always verify with your local recycling guidelines when in doubt.

Why do I need to sign in with Google?

Google sign-in provides a secure authentication method and allows us to sync your recycling history across devices. We only access basic profile information (email and name) and never post to your account.

Is my data shared with third parties?

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We use trusted service providers to help operate our app, but they are bound by strict confidentiality agreements. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account and all associated data from the Profile screen in the app. Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your information from our servers.

Does the app work offline?

Basic features like viewing your recycling history and guides work offline. However, the AI recognition feature requires an internet connection to process images and provide accurate results.

Troubleshooting Tips

Camera Issues

  • Ensure you've granted camera permissions to the app
  • Make sure you have adequate lighting when taking photos
  • Try restarting the app if the camera is unresponsive

Login Problems

  • Verify you have an active internet connection
  • Ensure your Google account is active and accessible
  • Try clearing the app cache and data
  • Update to the latest version of the app

App Performance

  • Close other apps running in the background
  • Ensure your device has sufficient storage space
  • Update to the latest version of the app
  • Restart your device if experiencing persistent issues
