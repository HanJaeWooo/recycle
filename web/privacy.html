Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Your Privacy Matters: RN Recycle is committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google OAuth, we collect:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your name and profile picture (if provided by Google)
  • Unique Google user identifier

1.2 App Usage Data

To provide our recycling services, we collect:

  • Photos you take for recycling identification (processed locally and on our servers)
  • Recycling history and scan results
  • App preferences and settings
  • Usage analytics (anonymized)

1.3 Device Information

We may collect basic device information:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version and crash reports
  • General location (for finding nearby recycling centers)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide Services: Identify recyclable materials, maintain your recycling history
  • Improve Experience: Personalize recommendations and improve our AI models
  • Account Management: Authenticate your account and sync data across devices
  • Support: Respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • Analytics: Understand app usage patterns to improve our services

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (Google Cloud, analytics providers)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition (with notice to users)

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Services

We use Google OAuth for authentication and Google Cloud services for data processing. Your use of Google services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4.2 YouTube API

We use YouTube Data API to show educational recycling videos. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure cloud storage with access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited employee access to personal data

6. Data Retention

We retain your information:

  • Account Data: Until you delete your account
  • Recycling History: For as long as you use the app
  • Photos: Processed immediately and not permanently stored
  • Analytics: Anonymized data may be retained longer for service improvement

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Update or correct your information
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Download your recycling history data
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions at any time

8. Children's Privacy

RN Recycle is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

9. International Users

If you are located outside the United States, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where our servers are located.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy in the app
  • Sending an email notification (if you've provided an email)
  • Displaying a notice in the app

12. Consent

By using RN Recycle, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our services.

Questions? We're here to help. Contact us at davidsuballa@gmail.com if you have any concerns about your privacy or data.
